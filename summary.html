Summary & Conclusions

This page provides an overview of key findings from the global streaming trends analysis and what can be done in the future.

  • Listener engagement is strongest among ages 25–44, with noticeable differences in genre preferences across age groups; this suggests that artists can maximize growth by targeting core millennial audiences while strategically aligning genre and marketing efforts to reach both younger and older listeners, balancing focused audience appeal with opportunities for cross-generational expansion.
  • Premium listeners tend to spend more time streaming music than free users across all age groups, especially among younger listeners. This suggests that artists can focus on turning engaged listeners into premium users while using free listeners as a way to reach new audiences and grow their fan base.

These visualizations highlight meaningful differences in listener behavior across genres, demographics, subscription types, and geographic regions, while also revealing opportunities for deeper analysis. Future work could incorporate time-based trends to examine how engagement and repeat behavior evolve, particularly around new releases. Integrating more granular user-level data, such as listening sessions or playlist interactions, could further clarify patterns in retention and discovery. Expanding the geographic analysis to capture regional variation within countries and emerging markets would enhance understanding of audience growth. Additionally, applying predictive modeling or clustering techniques could help identify distinct listener segments and forecast which genres or artists are likely to gain traction, supporting more targeted marketing and release strategies.
