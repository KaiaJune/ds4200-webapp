Streaming Time of Day by Genre

This heatmap shows how streaming activity varies across music genres and times of day, with an age group filter for comparison. The x axis represents morning, afternoon, and night, while the y axis shows the top genres.

Key insight: Nighttime is the most dominant listening period across many genres, especially for younger users. Patterns also shift across age groups, showing that when people listen depends on both genre and demographic behavior.
Time ranges: Morning = 6am to 12pm, Afternoon = 12pm to 6pm, Night = 6pm to 12am.
