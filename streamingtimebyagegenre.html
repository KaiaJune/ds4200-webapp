Streaming Time of Day by Genre

This heatmap compares when listeners are most active across music genres. Use the age-group filter to see how morning, afternoon, and nighttime listening patterns shift across different groups.

How to read this: darker blue cells indicate a larger share of listeners for that genre during that time of day. Percentages are calculated within each genre for the selected age group, which makes differences easier to compare.
Time ranges: Morning = 6am–12pm, Afternoon = 12pm–6pm, Night = 6pm–12am.
