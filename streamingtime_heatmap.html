Streaming Time of Day vs. Platform

This D3 heatmap shows how listening activity is distributed across streaming platforms and times of day. Instead of only using raw counts, it displays the percentage of each platform’s listeners who are most active in the morning, afternoon, or night, making differences in daily behavior much easier to compare.

How to read it: darker blue cells indicate a larger share of that platform’s listeners during that time period. Time ranges are labeled directly on the x-axis: Morning (6am–12pm), Afternoon (12pm–6pm), and Night (6pm–12am).
