Future work could incorporate time-based trends to examine how engagement and repeat behavior evolve, particularly around new releases. Integrating more granular user-level data, such as listening sessions or playlist interactions, could further clarify patterns in retention and discovery. Expanding the geographic analysis to capture regional variation within countries and emerging markets would enhance understanding of audience growth. Additionally, applying predictive modeling or clustering techniques could help identify distinct listener segments and forecast which genres or artists are likely to gain traction, supporting more targeted marketing and release strategies.
