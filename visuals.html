We begin by analyzing trends in genre by age group

Trends Between Age & Top Genre

We begin our analysis by looking at how different age groups view music genres. The chart below can be filtered to five different age groups. With each filter, the relationship for each genre is shown.

From this chart, we can determine a few trends: teens have a strong affinity for classical music. Those aged 46-60 have the most even split of top genres. However, what stands out from this chart is the relationship between 18-25 year olds and EDM music. The chart shows their relationship is the strongest compared to all other groups.

Inside the 18-25 age group, 14% of users had EDM as their top genre. This is a higher percentage than any other age group & top genre pair, with the next highest being 12.5% of those aged 26-35 liking Jazz music.

Overall, the top genres per age group (Teen, 18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-60) are Classical, EDM, Jazz, Reggae, and Classical, respectively.

Repeat Song Rate Among Top Genres

Next, we can analyze if more popular genres tend to have users that repeat songs most often. And, which genres tend to elicit repeat song behavior.

We begin by analyzing teenagers. Their top genre was Classical music. Of all of the genres, teenagers had the highest repeat song rate for EDM and country music, rather than classical. Next, looking at those aged18-25, their top genre was EDM and they had a very strong relationship. Despite this, the repeat song rate was highest for Metal music. Those aged 26-35 most preferred jazz music. However, the repeat song rate for jazz is actually one of the lowewst in the age group. Their top repeat song rates come from R&B and Country. For the 36-45 age group, they had the highest repeat song rate for country, metal and rock. And finally, the 46-60 age group had similar repeat song rates accross the board.

One might expect the genre groups that have more fans would have a higher repeat song rate, however this has proven to not be true. Repeat song rate seems to depend on the specific genre that users like. Country and metal music had generally the highest repeat song rates. This means these genres may have users repeating songs more often.

Discover Weekly Engagement Trends

Next, we can analyze how age and genre impacts the weekly engagement with the discover applications.

>

When looking at the discover weekly engagement trends accross all age groups, there is no significant trend. However, when filtering to specific age groups, some trends appear. First looking at teenagers, those who prefer Hip-Hop and Reggae have the highest weekly engagement. Hip-hop continues to have the highest weekly engagement for the 18-25 age group. Those aged 26-35 that liked Jazz had the highest engagement. R&B was the highest for 36-45 year olds. And finally, the 46-60 age group had the highest engagement among those who like Rock music.

There appears to be no strong correlation between more popular genres and higher daily weekly engagement. There also seems to be no connection between repeat song rate and daily weekly engagement. However, within specific age groups there are genres with higher engagement. The biggest trend is that those aged 25 and under who prefer Hip-Hop music engage in weekly engagement the most.
