Streaming Time of Day vs Platform

This heatmap compares when listeners are most active across streaming platforms. Cell color shows the share of each platform’s listeners in a given time period, making differences easier to spot than raw counts alone.

How to read this: brighter green cells indicate a larger share of listeners for that platform during that time window. Percentages are calculated within each platform, so you can compare daily listening patterns more clearly across services.
Time ranges used: Morning = 6am–12pm, Afternoon = 12pm–6pm, Night = 6pm–12am.
This version uses within-platform percentages so differences in time-of-day behavior are easier to interpret.
